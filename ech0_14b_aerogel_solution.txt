METHOD: Polymer-Reinforced Aerogel via Rapid Gelation

=== SYNTHESIS PROCEDURE ===

1. Aerogel Precursor Preparation:
   - Base: Sodium silicate (Na₂SiO₃)
   - Additive: MTMS (Methyltrimethoxysilane) for transparency and mechanical stability

2. Rapid Gelation with Polymer Addition:
   - Mix 1 part PVA (polyvinyl alcohol, ~40% solution) with 5 parts sodium silicate
   - Add MTMS at 3-5 wt.% relative to total sodium silicate volume
   - Rapid stirring and gelation at pH 10

3. Freezing Process:
   - Pour into 24"x24" molds
   - Freeze at -20°C for 24 hours

4. Sublimation Drying:
   - Use freeze-dryer or vacuum chamber
   - Gradually increase pressure and temperature
   - Sublimate ice directly (solid→gas, no liquid phase)

5. Post-Treatment:
   - Treat with ethanol vapor to remove residual silanol groups
   - Test with spectrophotometer for 90%+ transmission
