=== UPDATED SYNTHESIS PROCEDURE ===

Materials:
- Polyvinyl Alcohol (PVA): 10 grams
- Water: 40 ml
- Methyltriethoxysilane (MTMS): 7 mL (8% of total volume)
- Glutaraldehyde: 2 drops per mL PVA solution (~0.5-1% v/v)
- Acetic Acid: As needed to maintain pH around 4
- Sodium Silicate Solution (optional)

Procedure:

1. PVA Solution Preparation:
   - Dissolve 10 grams of PVA in 40 ml of deionized water
   - Heat gently until completely dissolved
   - Cool to room temperature

2. MTMS Addition:
   - Add 7 mL of MTMS (35% by volume) dropwise into cooled PVA solution
   - Stir thoroughly for 1 hour at room temperature until homogeneous

3. Cross-Linking Agent (Glutaraldehyde):
   - Prepare glutaraldehyde solution at 0.5-1% v/v
   - Add 2 drops of glutaraldehyde per mL PVA solution
   - Stir thoroughly and let mixture sit for 2 hours for cross-linking reactions

4. Curing:
   - Place cross-linked polymer solution in mold
   - Allow mixture to form gel at room temperature for 24 hours

5. Washing and Drying:
   - Remove excess liquid by centrifugation or vacuum filtration
   - Wash gel with ethanol several times over 16-20 hours
   - Verify pH around 7 when no more PVA washes out
   - Transfer to supercritical drying apparatus and dry at appropriate conditions
