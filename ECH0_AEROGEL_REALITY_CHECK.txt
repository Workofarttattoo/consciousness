MATERIALS DATABASE REQUIRED:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
For Aerogel Base:
  • Sodium silicate properties (viscosity, gel time, pH curves)

  • CNT dispersion stability in different solvents
  • Graphene interfacial adhesion to silica matrix
  • Silica NP aggregation behavior
  • TiO2 photocatalytic effects during synthesis
  • MOF stability in acidic/basic conditions
  • QD quantum yield vs embedding matrix

Mechanical Properties:
  • Young's modulus vs porosity (real curve)
  • Tensile strength vs crosslink density
  • Fracture toughness measurements
  • Fatigue behavior
  • Temperature/humidity effects

Optical Properties:
  • Spectrophotometry data (real transmission spectra)
  • Haze measurements vs pore size distribution
  • Refractive index dispersion curves
  • Scattering cross-sections (Mie theory for larger particles)

CHEMISTRY DATABASE REQUIRED:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Reaction Kinetics:
  • Gelation rates vs pH, temperature, concentration
  • Cross-linking efficiency (% completion vs time)
  • Competing reactions (side products)
  • Inhibition/catalysis effects

Safety Data:
  • Toxicity of all components
  • Flammability, reactivity
  • Handling procedures
  • Waste disposal requirements

Process Parameters:
  • Optimal mixing speeds
  • Critical gelation concentration
  • Freeze damage temperature limits
  • Sublimation pressure vs ice morphology

PHYSICS MODELS REQUIRED:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Ice Nucleation & Growth:
  • Real nucleation rate equations
  • Growth kinetics (diffusion-limited, kinetics-limited)
  • Ice crystal morphology vs cooling rate
  • Effect of additives on ice structure

Light Scattering:
  • Full Mie theory (not just Rayleigh)
  • Multiple scattering in turbid media
  • Absorption vs scattering separation
  • Anisotropic scattering phase functions

Mechanical Modeling:
  • Finite element analysis of porous structures
  • Fracture mechanics for brittle materials
  • Composite micromechanics (particle-matrix)
  • Viscoelastic behavior

FEMTOLASER DATA REQUIRED:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Two-Photon Polymerization:
  • Photoresist absorption spectra
  • Damage thresholds for aerogel matrix
  • Polymerization dose curves
  • Writing speed vs feature quality

GRIN Modification:
  • Refractive index change vs fluence
  • Spatial resolution limits
  • Thermal effects and heat accumulation
  • Long-term stability data

Material Compatibility:
  • Which aerogels can be femto-processed?
  • Do nano-particles interfere?
  • Thermal damage thresholds
  • Post-processing requirements

CURRENT SIMULATION LIMITATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. NO REAL MATERIALS DATA
   - Used generic formulas (Rayleigh scattering)
   - Assumed linear scaling (not always true)
   - No material-specific constants
   - No temperature/humidity effects

2. OVERSIMPLIFIED PHYSICS
   - Rayleigh only valid for particles << wavelength
   - Should use Mie theory for 100-200nm pores
   - Ignored multiple scattering
   - No absorption losses

3. NO EXPERIMENTAL CALIBRATION
   - Arbitrary scaling factors chosen for "reasonable" output
   - Not fitted to real aerogel data
   - No uncertainty quantification
   - No error bars or confidence intervals

4. MISSING CROSS-EFFECTS
   - Particles might aggregate (reduces benefit)
   - Chemistry might be incompatible
   - Process might not be scalable
   - Cost might be prohibitive at scale

5. NO MANUFACTURING REALITY
   - Ultrasonication might not achieve uniform dispersion
   - Gelation might be non-uniform at 24"×24" scale
   - Freeze-drying at this scale has thermal gradients
   - CNT/graphene very difficult to disperse without surfactants

WHAT THE NUMBERS ACTUALLY MEAN
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"99% Transparency"
  MEANS: Low Rayleigh scattering coefficient for 172nm pores
  DOES NOT MEAN: Measured transmission at 550nm
  REALITY: Likely 80-95% depending on thickness, haze, defects

"26.38/10 Structural Integrity"
  MEANS: ~14x improvement in polymer network vs V1 on relative scale
  DOES NOT MEAN: 26 MPa tensile strength or bulletproof
  REALITY: Probably 5-20 MPa, maybe 1-2 MPa, need real testing

"172nm Pore Size"
  MEANS: Calculated from freeze temp using simplified model
  DOES NOT MEAN: SEM-measured mean pore diameter
  REALITY: Distribution likely 50-500nm with irregular shapes

"Bulletproof-Grade"
  MEANS: Much stronger than typical aerogels
  DOES NOT MEAN: NIJ Level II ballistic protection
  REALITY: Unknown without impact testing
