## 🔍 EMAIL FINDING TOOLS (Use These)

### Free Tools:
1. **LinkedIn** (best for finding names + titles)
   - Search: "Company Name" + "Job Title"
   - Look at profile, infer email from company format

2. **Hunter.io** (free: 25 searches/month)
   - Enter domain (e.g., pfizer.com)
   - Shows email format + finds addresses
   - URL: hunter.io

3. **RocketReach** (free: 5 lookups/month)
   - Enter name + company
   - Returns email + phone
   - URL: rocketreach.co

4. **Company Websites**
   - Check: /about, /team, /leadership, /press
   - Investor relations pages often have exec emails

## 📧 EMAIL FORMAT PATTERNS

Most companies follow these patterns:

1. **firstname.lastname@company.com** (70% of companies)
   - Example: john.smith@pfizer.com

2. **flastname@company.com** (20% of companies)
   - Example: jsmith@company.com

3. **firstname@company.com** (5% - startups)
   - Example: john@insitro.com

4. **firstname_lastname@company.com** (5%)
   - Example: john_smith@company.com

**How to verify:**
- Use Hunter.io to check company email pattern
- Try variations with email verification tool (NeverBounce, ZeroBounce)

## 🚨 IMPORTANT: Email Deliverability

### Before sending 20 cold emails:

1. **Warm up inventor@aios.is**
   - Send 5-10 emails to friends first
   - Have them reply
   - Builds sender reputation

2. **Use SMTP relay service**
   - SendGrid (free: 100 emails/day)
   - Mailgun (free: 5,000 emails/month)
   - Prevents Gmail blocking

3. **Personalize each email**
   - Change first line per prospect
   - Mention their recent work/publication
   - Don't blast generic template

4. **Send in batches**
   - Day 1: 5 emails
   - Day 2: 5 emails
   - Day 3: 10 emails
   - Avoids spam flags
