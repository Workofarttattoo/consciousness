=== SYNTHESIS PROCEDURE V3 ===

Ingredients:

- Glutaraldehyde: 1.2% v/v
- Carbon Nanotubes (CNTs): 0.3 wt%
- Deionized water, ethanol, reagents as needed

Procedure:

1. Preparation of Gel Solution:
   - Use V2 base recipe (10g PVA in 40ml water, 7 mL MTMS)

2. Glutaraldehyde Addition (1.2% v/v):
   - Increase concentration to 1.2% v/v
   - Mix thoroughly for uniform distribution

3. Carbon Nanotube Reinforcement (NEW):
   - Dissolve CNTs at 0.3 wt% relative to total dry silica mass
   - Use sonicator/ultrasound bath for uniform dispersion
   - Inspect under microscope - no agglomerates allowed

4. Gel Formation:
   - Add modified solution dropwise to base gel while stirring
   - Maintain 60°C, stir until desired viscosity reached

5. Gel Conditioning:
   - Degas to remove air bubbles
   - Optimize vacuum and temperature profiles

6. Curing Process:
   - Place in molds
   - Cure at 25°C for 1 hour
   - Then 12-14 hours overnight for thorough cross-linking

7. Freezing and Supercritical Drying:
   - Freeze at -80°C with programmed cycle to room temp
   - Supercritical CO2 drying with optimized parameters

Post-Processing:
- Spectrophotometry for transparency (400-700 nm)
- Compressive strength tests for structural integrity
